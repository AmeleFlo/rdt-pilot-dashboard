Research question

Is there a significant discordance between malaria RDT positivity rates counted manually and those reported in health facility registers and DHIS2 — and can monthly verification improve data accuracy and rational ACT use?

Cameroon's public health system spans 10 regions, 181 health districts and roughly 2,260 public health facilities (Cameroon Health Sector Strategy 2016–2027). This pilot covers 16 facilities in one health district — a small, geographically concentrated test of the verification approach before any wider scale-up.

Why this pilot

A 2023 baseline review found national test-positivity rates (TPR) as high as 89% in some regions against 60% elsewhere, and CHW-reported TPR averaging 83% — well above facility-level rates. Key informant interviews raised the possibility of over-reporting, partly linked to reporting incentives tied to positive case counts. This pilot was designed to test whether keeping and re-counting RDT cassettes, cross-checked monthly against routine reporting, can detect and correct that discordance.

How it works

Instead of discarding used RDT cassettes, the 16 pilot facilities (and participating CHWs) retain them for the month. Each month, cassettes are (1) counted manually and (2) re-read with the Audere HealthPulse digital reader, then compared against three routine reporting sources for the same period. Discrepancies are reviewed at a monthly validation meeting with facility and district staff, and corrective actions are assigned and followed up the next month.

Data sources in this dashboard

SourceWhat it is

Outpatient register (March only)Used as a stand-in comparator for March 2026 only, before the RMA indicator pairs below were clarified for the pilot.

The two RMA / DHIS2 indicator pairs

RMA and DHIS2 each report malaria testing through two different indicator pairs, which don't always move together. The dashboard lets you toggle between them wherever you see an "indicator pair" selector.

PairNumerator / Denominator
Ind.3 / Ind.5+7Confirmed simple + severe malaria cases treated (Ind.5+7) ÷ suspected cases tested (Ind.3).
Ind.21 / Ind.22RDT-confirmed cases (Ind.22) ÷ RDTs performed (Ind.21).

Both pairs exist in both RMA and DHIS2; the two can diverge within the same source and period.
